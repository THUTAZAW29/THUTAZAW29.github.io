01 / GAME OVERVIEW

2D SHOOTER, BUILT IN UNITY.

Dodge, shoot,
survive.

A small spacecraft moves through space, facing obstacles and enemies as they appear. The HUD shows energy and health, while the combat situation changes as the stage progresses.

The game flow includes a title menu, pause state, major enemy encounters and a mission-complete screen.

01 / PLAYER CONTROLLER

Movement, boost &
health / energy

The Player Controller exposes settings for movement speed, boost power, maximum energy, energy recovery and health. Energy and health bars appear at the top of the game screen.
